01 / About me

Organised thinking.
Practical follow-through.

A background spanning detailed international product workflows and hands-on New Zealand operations.

I like being the person who can bring clarity to a busy process: reviewing information, spotting what needs attention, keeping people updated and making sure actions don't get lost along the way.

At Brandix Apparel Solutions, I maintained product and costing records, tracked customer approvals and worked across suppliers and internal teams. At Big Barrel, I reviewed daily sales reports to help inform stock decisions, coordinated daily operations and supported inventory accuracy and online orders.

Those experiences have shown me how good information, dependable systems and thoughtful communication help a business make better decisions.

Why AA Home? The Business Coordinator opportunity combines what I enjoy most: dependable reporting, accurate business information, working across teams and turning everyday operational challenges into practical improvements.
